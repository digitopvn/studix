# Rain Check

## S01 - EXT. CAFE STREET - DUSK (RAIN)

Rain hammers the awning of a small corner cafe. MAI (30s) hurries along the
pavement under a red umbrella and snaps it shut at the door.

## S02 - INT. CAFE - DUSK

LINH (30s) waits at a window table, two cups already poured.

LINH
You came.

MAI
I said I would. I always do, eventually.
(beat)
Even when it rains.

LINH
Three years, Mai. Three years of "eventually".
I kept your seat warm the whole time.

Linh lifts her cup, not drinking. Mai takes the hit.

MAI
Then let me buy the next one.

## S03 - EXT. CAFE STREET - NIGHT

The rain has stopped. They step out together.

LINH
It stopped raining.

MAI
It always does.
(turning to her)
Same time next week?

LINH
Don't make me wait three years.

They walk away down the glistening street.
